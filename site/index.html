About Sanyo Aquarium

Sanyo Aquarium (Pte) Ltd exports ornamental freshwater fish, marine fish and invertebrates, aquatic plants and potted plants from Singapore, according to the company profile on its original website.

Quality and quarantine, as the company describes it Historical statement

  • Visual checks on all fishes from its suppliers, and rejection of fishes that do not meet quality standards.
  • Imported fishes held in quarantine, fed and treated during that period.
  • In-house conditioning on the shipment day before packing, with automated packing and sealing.

Summarised from the original profile (last modified 10 July 2012); not re-verified. Figures, memberships and support promises are on the company page with the same qualification.
