Company profile

Sanyo Aquarium (Pte) Ltd is a Singapore exporter of ornamental freshwater fish, marine fish and invertebrates, aquatic plants and potted plants.

About these statements. This profile is rewritten from the company’s original website, whose pages were last modified on 10 July 2012. Statements marked Historical statement are quoted from that site, have not been re-verified and are not presented as current facts. The company is asked to confirm or update them. See the content notes.

What the company exports

Main products and services as listed on the original profile:

  • Export of local freshwater fishes including Platies, Swordtails, Angels, Guppies, Colisa, Mollies etc.
  • Export of imported fishes from Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand etc.

The catalogue also covers aquatic plants, plants on driftwood and driftwood — see the full catalogue.

Historical statement “24-hrs customer support” — no operating hours or response times are published, and none are promised here.

Quality and quarantine Historical statement

“As a major exporter, we are able to fulfill orders of varying types of fishes or plants and of varying quantities. We have instituted quality controls and quarantine techniques that meet the most stringent import requirements of the destination countries.”
Original company profile

The original profile lists these quality-control processes:

  • Visual checks on all fishes from our suppliers
  • Reject fishes that do not meet ours and customers' quality standards
  • Imported fishes are kept in quarantine for 2 weeks in our quarantine areas (There are over 600 holding tanks in our quarantine house)
  • Fishes are fed with high quality feed and treatment during the quarantine period
  • Conditioning the fishes using our in-house treatment on the shipment day prior to packing for export
  • Automated packing technology and sealing machines to ensure that the fishes reached the customers in optimum condition

Quarantine duration and tank numbers are the company’s own figures from the original website and have not been confirmed for today.
