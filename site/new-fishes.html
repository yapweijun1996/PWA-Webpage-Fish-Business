Two named entries from the original site’s “New Fishes” page.

Puntius denisoni

Name as written on the original site: PUNTIUS DENISONI

Proposed correction (not confirmed by the company): Puntius denisonii. The usual spelling is “denisonii”, and the genus may since have been revised. Not confirmed by the company.

pH
6.5 - 7.5
Temperature
18 - 25 °C

Description (original text)

A very impressive looking barb.

There are two colour forms, they have a vivid red line and the difference being that in one colour form the black line starts at the tip of the snout with more vivid colouration.

Their maximum size is around 10cm whilst the larger form achieves a size of around 14 to 15cm.

Botia kubotia

Name as written on the original site: BOTIA KUBOTIA

Proposed correction (not confirmed by the company): Botia kubotai. The usual spelling is “kubotai”. Not confirmed by the company.

pH
6.8 - 7.3
Temperature
23 - 27 °C

Description (original text)

A very social fish, it is best to keep them in groups of three or more.

Numerous shelters should be provided which gives them greater security and generally makes them bolder.

They are an active loach and can be seen hovering around the upper levels.

This species is very variable in pattern and can change quite dramatically as it grows.

Generally, the dark areas gradually increase in size as the fish matures.
