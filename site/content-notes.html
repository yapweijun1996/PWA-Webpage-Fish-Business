Content notes

How the original website’s content was carried over, and what still needs company review.

For reviewers. This page is part of the review hand-over. It lists every place where the rebuild keeps, qualifies or withholds original wording. It can be removed or trimmed once the company has confirmed the items.

Source and capture

  • Source: sanyoaqm.com.sg (opens in a new tab) ↗, audited on 2026-10-02.
  • 19 linked HTML pages and 132 image files were captured (the original files are kept unchanged in the delivery package).
  • Every page reports a last-modified date of 10 July 2012; the footer reads “Copyright © 2006”. Nothing on the source gives prices, stock, SKUs, minimum orders or arrival dates, and none are added.
  • Original spellings are kept exactly (for example “Cichild”, “XIPHOPHORUS HELERI”, “PUNTIUS DENISONI”, “BOTIA KUBOTIA”). Proposed corrections are always labelled as proposals and are not confirmed.
  • Photos with no individual label are identified only by their source album and original file name.

Review log (30 items, 21 awaiting company review)

R01 Company age claim Needs company review

Original text / anchor
With a history of over 37 years (aboutus.html)
Issue
Undated statement. Every source page was last modified on 10 July 2012 and the footer reads “Copyright © 2006”, so the number cannot be assumed to be current.
Treatment in this site
Shown only in the clearly qualified “Historical statements” section of the Company page, attributed to the original website. Not used in the hero or other marketing copy. No current years-in-business figure is calculated or implied.

R02 Long-standing customers and destination list (includes Cairo) Needs company review

Original text / anchor
buying from us for over 35 years (aboutus.html)
Issue
Undated relationship claim. The same paragraph lists “Cairo” among countries; Cairo is a city, not a country.
Treatment in this site
Quoted as written inside the qualified section. “Cairo” is reproduced exactly and is not relabelled as a country; a footnote states that the list needs company review. Not presented as a current customer list and not used as a shipping-destination promise.

R03 OFI / SAFEA membership Needs company review

Membership statement and logos are from the legacy site; current membership has not been verified with either organisation.
Treatment in this site
Statement and logos appear only in the qualified section of the Company page, labelled “not verified as current”. Logos are not used in the header or footer.

R07 Platy breeding temperature (unit error) Needs company review

Original text / anchor
do best at about 74 Degree Celsius (freshwaterfishes_platy.html, freshwaterfishes_platy_2.html)
Issue
The text gives a breeding range of “65 to 90” and an optimum of “74 Degree Celsius”. 74 °C would kill the fish, so the unit is apparently wrong (the numbers look like Fahrenheit), but the correct unit is not confirmed. Page 2 repeats the paragraph with a different misspelling (“Celcious”).
Treatment in this site
The breeding paragraph is withheld from the catalogue pages; only the first descriptive sentence is shown. The verbatim original is kept in data/source-record.json and quoted, flagged, on the Content notes page. Nothing is silently corrected and no replacement figure is published.

“Platies are bred very much the same as Guppies; except that they are a little less likely to eat their young. Temperature to breed platies usually range from 65 to 90, but do best at about 74 Degree Celsius.”

Page 2 wording: “Platies are bred very much the same as Guppies; except that they are a little less likely to eat their young. Temperature to breed platies usually range from 65 to 90, but do best at about 74 Degree Celcious.”

Original text — flagged as likely erroneous. Do not rely on it.

R30 AI-generated illustration variants Handled in this rebuild

Original text / anchor
AI illustration ((catalogue image cards, home and category thumbnails, lightboxes, company/contact images and enquiry lines))
Issue
Illustrative variants could be mistaken for factual photographs of the company's stock if their status or provenance is unclear.
Treatment in this site
All 102 catalogue photos have separate accepted AI illustration variants. After the user asked for the building and holding-tank images to be regenerated as well, seven building/facility photos also received separate AI illustration variants. The catalogue run records 161 generation attempts: 102 accepted photo records and 59 rejected retries; the seven company/facility variants are accepted. Codex CLI Imagen was unavailable, so the user-requested built-in image_gen.imagegen tool generated the variants; it reported no model name or version. AI files are stored separately from original source images and delivered original WebPs. Short visible AI illustration badges, AI-specific alt text and concise enquiry labels identify the variants; a site-wide note says they are visual aids, not photos of actual stock or company premises. Generic exterior artwork does not depict Sanyo Aquarium's premises. The AI variants are the display default; switching to original or both mode preserves access to factual photographs. Company and association logos remain original.
